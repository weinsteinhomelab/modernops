01 — About us

We've been at this a
long time.

Doggy Spa started because we kept seeing the same thing: rushed appointments, stressed dogs, and owners who felt like their pet was just another number. We wanted to be the opposite of that.

We're a small team. We know most of our regulars by name — theirs and their dog's. We take walk-ins when we can, but we'd rather give your dog the time they deserve than squeeze in one more appointment.

If your dog has had bad experiences at groomers before, we hear that a lot. Come meet us first. No pressure, no appointment needed for a quick hello.

02 — How it works

A visit, start to finish.

We keep it simple and calm. Here's what a typical appointment looks like from the moment you walk in.

1

Say hello

We greet your dog, talk through what you're after, and check their coat and skin before anything starts.

2

Bath & dry

A warm bath with shampoo matched to their coat, a gentle blow dry, and a full brush out — no cage dryers.

3

The groom

Cut, trim, and tidy suited to the breed. Nails, ears, and paws handled with care and no rushing.

4

Pick up

We walk you through how it went, flag anything we noticed, and send you both home happy.

08 — Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

For full grooms and spa treatments, yes — it keeps the day calm and gives your dog our full attention. We'll take walk-ins for nail trims when we have a gap, but calling ahead is always safer.

How long will it take?

Most grooms run one to three hours depending on size, coat, and condition. We don't use cage dryers or rush the process, so we'll give you a realistic pick-up time when you drop off.

My dog is anxious or reactive. Can you still help?

Very often, yes — it's a big part of what we do. Let us know ahead of time, and we'll go slow, break the groom into steps, and never force anything. If it's a lot for your dog on a given day, we'll stop and talk it through with you.

What about vaccinations?

We ask that dogs are up to date on their core vaccinations (including rabies) for the safety of every pet in the shop. Bring records on your first visit or have your vet send them over.

How do I pay, and do you take tips?

Cash and all major cards. Tips are never expected but always appreciated by the team — 100% goes to the groomer who worked with your dog.
