Ops Customers

Know who you work with and where every job stands.

  • Every customer with their phone, email, address and notes.
  • Requests from your website form show up on their own. Nothing gets lost in an inbox.
  • Log calls, texts and visits, so you always know who you talked to last.
  • See each customer's quotes and history on one page.

Ops Quotes

Send a clean, professional quote while the customer is still interested.

  • Save your services and prices once. Add them to any quote with a click.
  • One-time and monthly, quarterly or yearly prices on the same quote.
  • A PDF with your logo and your terms, emailed straight to the customer.
  • See which quotes are waiting, accepted or turned down.

Ops Inbox

Read and answer your business email next to your customers.

  • All of your business mailboxes on one screen.
  • Reply from the same place you keep your customers and quotes.
  • Pictures from senders stay hidden until you choose to show them.
